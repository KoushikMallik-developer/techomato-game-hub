Real-world assets for Midnight Pursuit
======================================
Bundled (see CREDITS.txt): CC0 Poly Haven lighting/sky images and an asphalt texture.
You can replace any of them with your own files of the same name.

  hdri/<tod>.hdr   lighting + reflections for each time of day (overcast, day, sunset, night)
                   equirectangular .hdr, 512x256 is enough (it is blurred for lighting)
  sky/<tod>.jpg    the visible sky: the UPPER HALF of an equirectangular sky photo, 2048x512
                   (night uses the built-in star sky)
  textures/asphalt_color.jpg, asphalt_normal.jpg, asphalt_roughness.jpg
                   road surface; one tile covers about 4 metres of road

Free CC0 sources: https://polyhaven.com/hdris  https://polyhaven.com/textures  https://ambientcg.com
Reload the page after adding files.
